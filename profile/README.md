## What We Do

Compound237 is an independent DevOps consulting practice built on one principle: **sustainable, compounding improvement**. The name is a nod to the math — improve by just 1% each day and you are 37× better by year's end. That philosophy shapes everything we do for our clients.

We partner with enterprise teams to modernize their engineering practices, accelerate delivery, and build the internal capability to keep improving long after the engagement ends.

## Engagement Model

We keep things straightforward:

- **Advisory** — Strategy, architecture reviews, and roadmap design for teams that need expert guidance without full-time help.
- **Embedded** — Working alongside your engineers sprint-by-sprint to deliver and enable simultaneously.
- **Accelerators** — Fixed-scope deliverables (e.g., GitHub Enterprise migration, Azure landing zone, CI/CD modernization) with clear outcomes and timelines.

## How We Work

```
Assess → Design → Build → Enable → Improve
```

Every engagement begins by understanding where you are, then sets a measurable baseline. We build in the open, document as we go, and always leave teams in a better position to own what was built — because the goal is your compounding improvement, not dependency on us.
